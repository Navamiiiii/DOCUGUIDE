Application for Birth Certificate
Service Description : Citizens can use this service to get Birth certificate
Eligibility: Citizens of India
Supporting Document:
NA
Application Fee : Rs.50 per copy
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 35
Delivery Time (Days) : Immediate
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant submits the form
2. Applicant will get the Acknowledgment
3. Department will process the application
4. Applicant will receive the Birth Certificate
